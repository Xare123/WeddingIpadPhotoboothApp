# Premium visual revision recommendation — read only

Premium visual signoff remains pending. The prior independent acceptance established bounded browser/controller behavior; it did not establish a finished premium design. The parent's critique is valid: saturated correctness markers dominate the photographs, the three near-white papers are too similar at selection scale, and the tiny footer decorations do not supply recognizable theme character. Fit preserves pixels but the current broad windows make portrait photos look like small islands. No product, native module, tests or harness were changed for this note. Freeze the existing candidate through its current native CI run before creating a new product candidate.

Use two separate evidence sets. Keep the four colored-edge/corner-marker JPEGs exclusively for correctness assertions: fit, swap, source identity and filter/crop travel. For visual approval use four clean synthetic JPEG variants without borders, labels or watermarks over the image; the fixture banner must remain outside the product. If these derive from the same generated scene, disclose that explicitly. Ideally generate or provide four owned fictional scenes/poses with a portrait/landscape mix to expose real composition weaknesses. Do not imply actual camera poses or private guest images. The generated scene and clean variants must never be silently substituted into correctness assertions. Both sets should render the same production layout and export pipeline.

Make the themes distinct through paper, framing and decoration outside photo windows, without altering photo color or applying theme overlays to faces:

| Theme | Minimal recognizable treatment | Suggested palette |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classic Ivory | Warm ivory paper, thin double keyline, generous symmetrical margins; centered serif names and small spaced date; one small understated ampersand or diamond, no organic art. | Paper #F6F0E4, ink #372D27, rule #B6A07C |
| Sage Botanical | Muted sage outer field with ivory photo mats; two asymmetric olive/eucalyptus branches extending along opposite outside margins, visible at normal preview size; restrained serif footer. | Field #DCE5D6, mat #FAF7EE, ink #2D4537, leaves #6D8367 |
| Coastal Sea Glass | Pale aqua field, ivory photo mats, three fine flowing coastal contour lines across the lower outside margin and one upper corner; slightly more open footer spacing. Avoid generic shell clip art. | Field #DBEBE7, mat #FBF8EF, ink #244C49, line #6B9F99 |

Keep decoration in a reserved outer band, never in a photo slot. A useful starting size is a branch/contour motif spanning 12–18% of output width and at least 20–30% of a side margin's height, rather than the current miniature footer strokes. At the ordinary 480px preview, motif strokes should remain roughly 1–1.5px and the main decorative silhouette recognizable. Do not make all three themes the same pale card plus a different tiny icon. Theme cards should show the actual four photos, plus enough outer paper/motif to distinguish them immediately. Preserve instant switching and the current selected outline; avoid an added confirmation step.

Fix slot geometry before encouraging crop. Current outputs are fixed at strip 1200×3600 and grid 2400×2100, with fit inside broad slots. A portrait image cannot both preserve every pixel and fill a landscape slot; no pan/zoom tuning resolves that constraint. Choose a stable slot aspect from the original sources once when opening a new composition, after EXIF orientation, and keep it stable through theme changes, swaps and individual retakes. A minimal rule is portrait 4:5, landscape 4:3, square 1:1, selected by the median of four oriented aspect ratios. Fit remains the default for every individual source, including outliers. Do not let device orientation change export geometry or existing recipes.

For four portrait sources, prefer a portrait 2×2 grid with portrait windows, rather than the current landscape sheet. Approximately 2100×2700 is a reasonable draft canvas; choose exact margins and footer after a clean proof. A true four-frame vertical strip with portrait windows needs a much taller output, approximately 1200×6200, if each photo is to remain legible and uncropped. If the physical 1:3 strip format is a hard product requirement, retain it explicitly as a compact proof and acknowledge its portrait-image size limitation; do not hide that limitation by silently filling. For landscape sources the existing 1200×3600 strip and 2400×2100 grid are much more natural. Exact dimensions are design proposals, not approved print specifications.

Changing canvas/slot geometry affects canonical recipe validation, native and Canvas rasterizers and export-size assertions. Introduce an explicit new composition geometry/version for new recipes; preserve existing v1 composites and legacy five-photo sessions exactly. Do not reinterpret an already saved recipe under new dimensions. Keep source IDs, four originals, filtering and per-photo crops unchanged; swap must continue to move the complete photo/crop/look identity.

Keep Fill as an explicit per-photo opt-in with a large selected-photo preview. Change the warning to concrete language such as “Fill may crop faces and edges. Your original stays unchanged.” Provide a single-tap Full photo reset beside it. The current pan arrows/zoom can remain initially; a full gesture editor is unnecessary for this bounded revision. A user should see the actual crop before saving. Offer no automatic face crop until it is separately designed and proven.

Make strip inspection useful without changing the exported recipe: tap the main preview to open a dismissible inspection sheet, with Whole keepsake and Actual size / fit width. Fit width should scroll vertically through the strip with names/date reachable, and a clear Done action should return to the same studio selection. Grid can use the same sheet with pinch/zoom optional. Keep default Save visible in the studio, and keep template/crop controls separate from inspection. No need for more layout choices or settings in the default flow.

Before renewed premium approval, show clean side-by-side exports and normal-size UI views for all six combinations, both device orientations, and portrait/landscape/mixed source groups. Check the decorative signature at thumbnail scale, legibility of each strip photo at fit width, no unrequested crop, no decorative intrusion into images, and initial visibility of Save, QR and Next guests. Keep marker-based correctness results alongside this evidence, labeled separately. Native CoreImage/CoreText and physical iPad results remain separate gates; Canvas proofs cannot approve Apple rasterization, color, font metrics or native glass.
