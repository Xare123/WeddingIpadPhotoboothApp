# Camera rehearsal card

Print [the one-page PDF](camera-check.pdf), or display [the PNG](camera-check.png) on another screen and photograph it with the actual booth camera. Keep the entire border and all four corner dots inside the captured original. The sentence should remain readable, with TOP above the arrow and LEFT on its left.

Inspect the untouched original in the host library, the separate Photos copy and, after live pairing/sharing is ready, a downloaded original on another phone. Compare text orientation, all four corners and the border. The live full-screen preview may crop its view; inspect the saved original using **Full photo**. An explicitly chosen **Fill** crop can intentionally remove edges.

This original vector card contains no guest photo or private information. Its gray patches are a rough visual aid, not calibrated color/brightness measurements. A rendered card is not evidence that the iPad camera, Photos export or a download has passed. Follow [the owner rehearsal](../../docs/OWNER_IPAD_HANDOFF.md) and preserve all existing app data.

[File hashes and generation details](camera-check-manifest.json) identify the PDF/PNG. The PDF embeds a DejaVu Sans font subset; its [font notice](FONT_LICENSE.txt) is also included in the PDF metadata. No new app dependency is required to use the card.
